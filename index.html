The Chronicles of Ember

Chapter I: The First Spark

Long before the lanterns were lit across the forest canopy, Ember arrived as a tiny ball of golden fur under a shooting star. With soft tabby stripes and eyes like emerald dew, she claimed every shaded nook as her personal kingdom.

Chapter II: The Whispering Pines

By day, she naps upon sunlit moss; by night, she dances with glowing fireflies. Every rustle in the undergrowth is an invitation to adventure, and every visitor who crosses her path is greeted with a gentle head-butt and a warm purr.

Chapter III: Found & Forever Home

Though she loves to wander far into the midnight woods, she always knows the way back to the cozy hearth. If you follow the pawprints, you will never truly be lost in Ember's realm.
